The instrument
Reading 02 · the protocol

Knowledge is developed, not generated.

Most tools hand you the first thing a model says. Here, every concept passes through the same instrument: an expert plans it, gathers primary sources, cross-checks them against each other, then writes the lesson. What can't be verified doesn't ship.

Decompose

The expert breaks a concept into the exact questions a rigorous lesson must answer.

Primary sources

It searches, then reads the authoritative source cleanly: filings, standards, seminal work.

Cross-check

Every load-bearing claim is corroborated across two independent sources; conflicts are logged.

The gate

Only a fully-sourced, verified lesson passes. Draft material is held back, visibly.

Reading 03 · the observers

Experts that deepen every time they're used.

A field expert

Owns a whole subject and holds a living profile of its sources, debates, and glossary.

Niche sub-experts

It delegates each niche to a specialist that researches deeper than any generalist would.

A pool that compounds

Everything found is pooled. Ask for an overlapping course and it reuses what's proven, never twice.
