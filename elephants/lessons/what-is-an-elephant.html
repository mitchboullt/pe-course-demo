What Is an Elephant

🤔 Guess First!

Try to guess before you read on — it's okay to get it wrong!

  1. How much do you think the biggest elephants weigh? (Hint: think about how many cars that might be!)
  2. Are elephants more closely related to hippos, or to a tiny animal called a hyrax that looks like a big guinea pig?
  3. How long do you think a wild elephant can live?

🐘 The Big Idea

An elephant is the biggest animal that lives on land anywhere on Earth. It has a long trunk, big floppy ears, thick wrinkly gray skin, and huge legs like tree trunks to hold up its giant body.

📖 Let's Learn More

There are three kinds (species) of elephant alive today: the African savanna elephant, the African forest elephant, and the Asian elephant. They all share the same basic elephant body plan — trunk, tusks, big ears — but they're different sizes and live in different parts of the world. You'll meet all three in the next note.

Elephants are mammals, just like dogs, whales, and humans. That means elephant moms feed their babies milk, and elephants breathe air with lungs. Believe it or not, an elephant's closest living relative isn't a hippo or a rhino — it's a small furry animal called a hyrax, which looks nothing like an elephant at all! Scientists figured this out by comparing DNA, the instruction code inside every living thing's cells.

🤯 Cool Facts

  • The biggest elephants can weigh as much as 2 or 3 pickup trucks put together.
  • Wild elephants can live 60 to 70 years — almost as long as a grandparent!
  • An elephant's skin can be over an inch thick in places, but it's actually sensitive enough to feel a fly landing on it.
  • Elephants can't jump. Not even a little hop! Their leg bones are stacked straight up like pillars, built for carrying huge weight, not for springing off the ground.

❓ Quiz Time

Q1. What kind of animal is an elephant — reptile, mammal, or fish?

Mammal! Elephant moms feed their babies milk, just like other mammals.

Q2. True or false: an elephant's closest relative is the hippo.

False — it's actually a small animal called a hyrax!

Q3. Can elephants jump?

No — their straight, pillar-like legs aren't built for jumping.

🎨 Try This!

Stand up and try to walk the way you think an elephant walks — slow, heavy steps, swinging your arms like a trunk. Now try to jump like one. (Trick question — you can't, but a real elephant can't either!)

🗣️ Explain It!

Tell a family member or a stuffed animal: what makes an elephant an elephant? Try to use the words mammal, trunk, and tusks without looking back at the note.

📚 New Words

Mammal
an animal that feeds its babies milk and usually has fur or hair.
Species
a specific kind of animal or plant (like "African savanna elephant" is one species).
Hyrax
a small furry animal that is actually the elephant's closest living relative.
