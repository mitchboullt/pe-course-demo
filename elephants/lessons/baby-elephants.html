Lesson 6 of 10

Baby Elephants

🤔 Guess First!

  1. How long do you think a mother elephant is pregnant before her baby is born?
  2. About how much do you think a newborn elephant calf weighs?
  3. Does a baby elephant know how to use its trunk right away, or does it need practice?

🐘 The Big Idea

Baby elephants, called calves, take longer to grow inside their mothers than any other animal on Earth — almost two whole years — and they're born already able to stand and walk within about an hour!

📖 Let's Learn More

An elephant mother is pregnant for about 18 to 22 months — the longest pregnancy (called a "gestation period") of any animal alive. That's almost double a human pregnancy! When the calf is finally born, it already weighs about 90–113 kg (200–250 lbs) — as much as a grown adult human, or more.

A newborn calf can stand and start walking within about an hour of being born, which it needs to do quickly to keep up with the herd. But even though it can walk right away, a calf is far from an expert elephant yet. It takes months of practice to learn to control its trunk — baby elephants often trip over their own trunks or accidentally step on them while learning!

Calves drink their mother's milk for several years, sometimes up to age 3 or later, even after they start eating plants too. And it's not just mom who helps raise a calf — other females in the herd, called "allomothers," pitch in to babysit, protect, and teach the calf as it grows up.

🤯 Cool Facts

  • A newborn calf is often taller than a 10-year-old kid!
  • Calves are born nearly blind and rely on touch, smell, and their mother's guidance at first.
  • Young calves sometimes suck on their trunks the way human babies suck their thumb.

❓ Quiz Time

Q1. About how long is an elephant mother pregnant?

About 18 to 22 months — the longest of any animal.

Q2. Can a newborn calf walk right away?

Yes — usually within about an hour of being born.

Q3. What's an "allomother"?

Another female in the herd (not the mom) who helps babysit and protect a calf.

🎨 Try This!

Count on your fingers how many months an elephant pregnancy lasts (18–22) versus how many months a human pregnancy lasts (about 9). How many times longer is the elephant's?

🗣️ Explain It!

Explain to a friend why it's actually really important for a baby elephant to be able to walk almost right away — what might happen if it couldn't?

📚 New Words
