Elephant Ears and How Elephants Talk

🤔 Guess First!

  1. Besides hearing, what's another job elephant ears do?
  2. Do you think elephants can "talk" to each other using sounds too low for humans to hear?
  3. How far away do you think an elephant call might be heard by other elephants?

🐘 The Big Idea

Elephant ears do double duty: they help elephants hear and also cool down. And elephants "talk" to each other using rumbles so deep that human ears often can't even hear them!

📖 Let's Learn More

An elephant's big ears are full of blood vessels — tiny tubes that carry blood close to the skin's surface. When an elephant flaps its ears, air moving over all those blood vessels cools the blood down, which then cools the whole elephant. That's why African elephants, who live somewhere hotter, evolved bigger ears than Asian elephants.

Elephants also communicate using infrasound — very low, deep rumbling sounds below the pitch that human ears can pick up. You'd feel it more like a vibration than hear it as a "sound." These rumbles can travel for miles across open land, letting elephant families that are far apart stay in touch, warn each other about danger, or find each other again. Elephants can even feel these rumbles through their sensitive feet, picking up vibrations traveling through the ground!

Elephants make other sounds too — trumpeting (that loud blaring sound you probably know from movies) when they're excited, scared, or angry, plus growls, snorts, and roars.

🤯 Cool Facts

  • Elephant rumbles can travel several kilometers, letting separated elephants stay connected.
  • Elephants can pick up ground vibrations through their feet, not just their ears.
  • A big flap of the ears can also be a warning signal — it can mean "back off!" if an elephant feels threatened.

❓ Quiz Time

Q1. Name two jobs that elephant ears do.

Hearing, and cooling the body down by releasing heat through blood vessels in the ears.

Q2. What is infrasound?

Extremely low, deep rumbling sounds — often too low for human ears to hear — that elephants use to communicate over long distances.

Q3. How can an elephant "hear" sounds through the ground?

Its feet are sensitive enough to feel vibrations traveling through the ground.

🎨 Try This!

Fan yourself with a piece of paper on a warm day and notice how moving air feels cooler on your skin — that's a tiny version of what an elephant's ear-flapping does, just much bigger!

🗣️ Explain It!

Explain to a friend: why would an animal that lives somewhere hot need giant ears, even if its hearing didn't need to be any better?

📚 New Words

Infrasound
extremely low-pitched sound, often too deep for human ears to hear.
Blood vessel
a tiny tube inside the body that carries blood around.
Vibration
a fast back-and-forth shaking movement that can travel through air or ground.
