Where Elephants Live

🤔 Guess First!

  1. Name a continent (or two) where you think wild elephants live today.
  2. Do elephants only live in hot, dry places, or in other kinds of habitats too?
  3. Do you think elephants stay in one small area their whole lives, or travel long distances?

🐘 The Big Idea

Wild elephants today live in parts of Africa and Asia — in grassy savannas, thick forests, and even near mountains — and many of them travel long distances every year following food and water.

📖 Let's Learn More

African savanna elephants roam wide, grassy plains dotted with trees across countries like Kenya, Tanzania, Botswana, and South Africa. African forest elephants live deep in the thick rainforests of Central Africa, like in the Congo Basin — they're smaller and harder to spot than their savanna cousins because of all the trees.

Asian elephants live in forests and grasslands across countries including India, Sri Lanka, Thailand, and Indonesia. Asia has far more people living close to elephant habitat than Africa does, which makes it harder for elephants there to find enough space to roam.

Many elephant herds migrate — travel long distances, sometimes hundreds of kilometers — following the seasons, moving toward water and fresh plants as the dry season turns into the wet season and back again. The matriarch's memory of these routes (from the Elephant Families and Herds note) is what makes this possible year after year.

🤯 Cool Facts

  • Some elephant herds migrate hundreds of kilometers across the year, following rainfall.
  • Elephants leave behind wide, worn paths through forests and grasslands from generations of the same migration routes — some paths have been used for hundreds of years.
  • Elephants can swim! They're actually strong swimmers and sometimes use their trunks as built-in snorkels while crossing rivers.

❓ Quiz Time

Q1. Name the two continents where wild elephants live today.

Africa and Asia.

Q2. What's the difference between where savanna elephants and forest elephants live?

Savanna elephants live on open grassy plains; forest elephants live in thick rainforest.

Q3. What does "migrate" mean?

To travel long distances, often following the seasons, in search of food and water.

🎨 Try This!

Find Africa and Asia on a world map or globe. Point to a country in each continent where elephants live (hint: Kenya in Africa, India in Asia).

🗣️ Explain It!

Explain to someone why elephant herds travel long distances instead of just staying in one spot all year.

📚 New Words

Habitat
the natural environment where an animal normally lives.
Migrate
to travel long distances, usually on a seasonal pattern, to find food, water, or better conditions.
Congo Basin
a huge rainforest region in Central Africa.
