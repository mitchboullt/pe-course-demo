What Do Elephants Eat

🤔 Guess First!

  1. Do you think elephants eat meat, plants, or both?
  2. About how many hours a day do you think an elephant spends eating?
  3. How much food do you think a full-grown elephant eats in one day?

🐘 The Big Idea

Elephants are herbivores, meaning they eat only plants — and because they're so enormous, they need to eat a huge amount of food almost all day long just to keep their giant bodies going.

📖 Let's Learn More

An adult elephant eats grass, leaves, bark, roots, and fruit — basically whatever plant food it can find nearby. To fuel a body that can weigh several tons, an elephant needs to eat up to 150 kg (330 lbs) of food a day — imagine eating your own body weight in salad every single day! Because of this, elephants spend as much as 16 hours a day — most of their waking life — eating or searching for food.

Elephants aren't very efficient digesters — a lot of what they eat passes through without being fully used, which actually helps the ecosystem, because their droppings spread seeds from the fruit they ate, helping new plants grow across the land they roam. That makes elephants important "gardeners" of their habitat.

The trunk does the heavy lifting here — grabbing bunches of grass, stripping leaves off branches, or pulling bark from trees, then delivering it all to the mouth.

🤯 Cool Facts

  • An adult elephant can drink up to 200 liters (about 50 gallons) of water in a single day.
  • Elephants sometimes use their tusks to strip bark off trees or dig for water underground.
  • Because elephants spread seeds through their droppings across huge distances, scientists call them "ecosystem engineers" — some plants even depend on elephants to spread their seeds!

❓ Quiz Time

Q1. Are elephants herbivores, carnivores, or omnivores?

Herbivores — they eat only plants.

Q2. Roughly how many hours a day does an elephant spend eating?

Up to about 16 hours a day.

Q3. How do elephants help plants grow in new places?

Seeds from the fruit they eat pass through and get spread across the land in their droppings.

🎨 Try This!

Look up how much you weigh, then imagine eating that much food in one day — that's roughly what a grown elephant does! Write down or say out loud how wild that sounds.

🗣️ Explain It!

Explain to a friend why elephants are sometimes called "gardeners" of their habitat, even though they're just eating and pooping like every other animal.

📚 New Words

Herbivore
an animal that eats only plants.
Ecosystem
all the living things (plants, animals) and their environment in one area, working together.
